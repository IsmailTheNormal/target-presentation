Laboratoriya Vazifasi: AES-256 Shifrlash va Yaxlitlik Testi

Node.js crypto kutubxonasida AES-256-GCM algoritmi bilan matnni shifrlash, IV va Auth Tag ni ajratish, hamda shifrlangan baytlarni ataylab o'zgartirib yaxlitlik tekshiruvini sinash.

Kriptografik BosqichDastur Kodi / BuyruqKutilgan NatijaHolat
1. Kalit Generatsiya`crypto.randomBytes(32)`256-bitli yuqori entropiyali kalit✅ Tayyor
2. AES-GCM Shifrlash`createCipheriv('aes-256-gcm', k, iv)`Ciphertext + Auth Tag yaratildi
3. Tamper Test (Buzish)`enc[0] = enc[0] === 'a' ? 'b' : 'a'``Unsupported state` xatosi (Rad etildi)
4. RSA OpenSSL`openssl genpkey -algorithm RSA`4096-bitli asimmetrik kalit juftligi
