Viktorina: Qaysi Koordinata Qahramonni Yuqori O'ngga Eltadi?

Sahna markazi (0, 0). Qahramonni eng yuqori va eng o'ng burchakka yuborish uchun qaysi blok kerak?

A) go to x: -200 y: -150 (Quyi chap burchak)
B) go to x: -200 y: 150 (Yuqori chap burchak)
C) go to x: 200 y: 150 (Yuqori o'ng: ikkala son ham musbat!)
D) go to x: 0 y: -180 (Markazning eng pasti)
To'g'ri javob: C!

Chunki o'ngga yurish musbat X (+200), tepaga chiqish esa musbat Y (+150) demakdir. Har ikkala qiymat 0 dan katta bo'lishi shart!
