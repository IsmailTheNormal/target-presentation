4. DRAM 1T1C Yacheykasi Zaryad Sizishi Matematikasi 1.5 Ball

4.1. DRAM yacheykasidagi kondensator sig'imi C = 30 fF (30 × 10⁻¹⁵ F), mantiqiy "1" kuchlanishi V = 1.2 V. Tranzistor orqali sizib chiquvchi mikrotok I_leak = 0.5 pA (0.5 × 10⁻¹² A). Δt = (C · ΔV) / I_leak formulasi bo'yicha kuchlanish ΔV = 0.6 V ga pasayib, bit yo'qolguncha qancha vaqt o'tishini hisoblang:

Δt = (30 × 10⁻¹⁵ F × 0.6 V) / (0.5 × 10⁻¹² A) = _______________________ soniya = ____________ millisekund.
Xulosa: Nega JEDEC standarti bo'yicha xotira har 64 ms da majburiy yangilanishi (Refresh) shart?
