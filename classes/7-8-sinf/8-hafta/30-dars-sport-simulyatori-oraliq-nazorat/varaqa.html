1 Karta matematikasi: Yarim himoyachi (CAM) OVR hisobi

O'yinchi ko'rsatkichlari: PAC=82, SHO=78, PAS=88, DRI=86, DEF=54, PHY=70. CAM pozitsiyasi vazn koeffitsientlari: PAS(0.30), DRI(0.25), SHO(0.20), PAC(0.15), DEF(0.05), PHY(0.05).

OVR = (PAS * 0.30) + (DRI * 0.25) + (SHO * 0.20) + (PAC * 0.15) + (DEF * 0.05) + (PHY * 0.05)
Hisoblash qatori: (88 * 0.30) + (86 * 0.25) + (78 * 0.20) + (82 * 0.15) + (54 * 0.05) + (70 * 0.05) = ?

O'yinchining yaxlitlangan yakuniy reytingi (Math.round): ____________ OVR
