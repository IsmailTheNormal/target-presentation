01 Pathfinding Nazariyasi: Kataklar va A* Formulalari
Pathfinding qidiruvining asosiy formulalari:
1. Katak koordinatasi: col = Math.floor(x / TILE_SIZE), row = Math.floor(y / TILE_SIZE)
2. A* formulasi: f(n) = g(n) + h(n) (g - qadamlar, h - evristika)
3. Manhattan masofasi: h = Math.abs(a.x - b.x) + Math.abs(a.y - b.y)
4. FSM holatlari: PATROL (1x tezlik), ALERT (shubha), CHASE (1.8x A* ta'qib)
