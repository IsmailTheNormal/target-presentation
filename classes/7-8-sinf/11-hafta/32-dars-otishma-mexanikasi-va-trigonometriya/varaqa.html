01 Otishma Fizikasining 4 Asosiy Ustuni (Trigonometriya va Vektorlar)
2D fazoda asosiy otishma formulalari:
1. Delta masofa: dx = mouseX - hero.x, dy = mouseY - hero.y
2. Burchak: angle = Math.atan2(dy, dx) (-PI dan +PI gacha radianlar)
3. Harakat vektorlari: vx = Math.cos(angle) * speed, vy = Math.sin(angle) * speed
4. Xotirani tozalash: for (let i = bullets.length - 1; i >= 0; i--) bullets.splice(i, 1);
